02 · Accounting operations

Full-cycle accounting,
automated where it counts.

Journal entries, reconciliations, and client invoicing on multi-million-dollar projects, in a compliance-heavy environment — and a layer of automation I built on top of all of it:

What I built

  • Invoice generator — Copilot-built VBA pipeline that prepares client invoices, materials and labour billing, and renders the finished PDFs
  • Journal-entry workbook — formula-driven, AI-assisted Excel workbook; entries pre-coded to paste cleanly into JD Edwards, manual-entry errors cut
  • Payroll-reporting macro — weekly and monthly client-job reports in about 5 minutes end to end; realistically an hour each by hand — I automated it from day one, so it was never done slow
  • Automated email distribution — reports and billing sent without touching an inbox
