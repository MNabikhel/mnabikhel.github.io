02 · Work

What I built, on the job.

Before PCL I was an Associate Accountant at CB&I in Fort Saskatchewan — supporting multi-million-dollar industrial projects with reporting, forecasting, and full-cycle accounting. Described in outline; the data stays confidential.

01Power BI · Copilot · VBA

Financial reporting & analytics

Executive reporting, monthly rhythm.

Managers across the company made decisions from reporting I owned end to end — each report built with a different mix of AI and hand-crafting, whichever the job deserved.

What I built

  • Overhead report — the tedious one, so I automated it with VBA: several hours of manual work down to about 10 minutes, monthly, company-wide
  • Executive summary report — built with Copilot, but every measure and its logic written by me: four quadrants — Operations, Finance, Sales, and Safety — each with its own KPIs
  • Precontract cost report — logic and code Copilot-drafted, directed and verified by me; tracks costs on jobs still being bid, before they're won or lost

02Excel VBA · JDE

Accounting operations

Full-cycle accounting, automated where it counts.

Journal entries, reconciliations, and client invoicing on multi-million-dollar projects, in a compliance-heavy environment — and a layer of automation I built on top of all of it:

What I built

  • Invoice generator — Copilot-built VBA pipeline that prepares client invoices, materials and labour billing, and renders the finished PDFs
  • Journal-entry workbook — formula-driven, AI-assisted Excel workbook; entries pre-coded to paste cleanly into JD Edwards, manual-entry errors cut
  • Payroll-reporting macro — weekly and monthly client-job reports in about 5 minutes end to end; realistically an hour each by hand — I automated it from day one, so it was never done slow
  • Automated email distribution — reports and billing sent without touching an inbox
